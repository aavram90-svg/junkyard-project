Prospa

  • Harvey Blumler
  • Guiorgi "Gosha" Smith

Prospa is an English electronic music duo consisting of Leeds born Harvey Blumler and Guiorgi "Gosha" Smith.

Free Your Mind
Prospa's debut album Free Your Mind is a high energy album of house and festival anthems that captures the duo's evolution from underground producers to main stage headliners

This Rhythm
"This Rhythm" is a standout single by Prospa featuring RAHH that heavily channels classic '90s UK house and nu-disco elements originally released via CircoLoco Records.
